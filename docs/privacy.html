Privacy Policy

Trading Poster · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Trading Poster ("the app") is a personal tool that its owner uses to publish posts to Threads accounts he owns or manages, and to view the statistics of those posts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we access

When a Threads account connects to the app through Meta's official login, the app may access, only for that connected account:

  • Basic profile information: Threads user ID, username, profile picture and bio (threads_basic).
  • The ability to create and publish posts on behalf of that account (threads_content_publish).
  • Insights for that account's own posts, such as views, likes, replies and reposts (threads_manage_insights).
  • Replies to that account's own posts, to read and manage them (threads_read_replies, threads_manage_replies), if granted.

The app does not access data of other Threads users beyond what Threads shows publicly on the connected account's own posts.

How we use it

  • To publish posts that the account owner has written and approved.
  • To show the account owner how his posts perform, so he can improve his content.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and post statistics are stored only locally on the owner's own computer. Images attached to posts are hosted in a public GitHub repository so Threads can download them; these images are published content and contain no personal data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. You can disconnect the app at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Trading Poster → Remove. After removal, the app can no longer access the account, and locally stored data for that account is deleted within 30 days. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
