Data Deletion Instructions

Trading Poster · Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can delete all data Trading Poster holds about your Threads account at any time.

Option 1. Remove the app in Threads

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings → Account → Website permissions (or Apps and websites).
  2. Find Trading Poster and tap Remove.

The app immediately loses access to your account. Your access token and any stored statistics for your account are deleted from the app within 30 days.

Option 2. Ask us

Send a message to Telegram @SyndicateTrdn with your Threads username and the words "delete my data". We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Posts you published on Threads stay on Threads; you can delete them in the Threads app.
